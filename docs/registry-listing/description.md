DinkusKit Accounts lets shoppers sign in to your store with a code sent to their email. No passwords to remember or leak.

- **Sign in with an emailed code**: a shopper enters their email and gets a 6-digit code. The code lasts 10 minutes and allows 5 tries. A shopper stays signed in for 30 days.
- **Saved delivery address and phone number**: a signed-in shopper saves one of each, and your checkout page fills them in.
- **Guest checkout stays**: shoppers never have to make an account to buy.
- **Your store's own email sender** sends the codes.

Shoppers are not admin users of your site: they sign in to your store, not to EmDash. Codes and sign-ins are kept only as one-way fingerprints, and expired codes and sign-ins are cleared twice a day.
