**Do shoppers need a password?**

No. They enter their email and type the 6-digit code it receives. A new code can be asked for after 30 seconds.

**Do shoppers have to sign in to buy?**

No. Guest checkout always works. Signing in only fills in the saved delivery address and phone number.

**Can shoppers see their past orders?**

Not yet. Until then, DinkusKit Commerce's order lookup finds an order by its number and email.

**Is a billing address saved?**

No. Checkout keeps "billing same as delivery" unless the shopper changes it.

**Where do the codes come from?**

From the sender you pick in Settings > Email, using your store's own address. DinkusKit never sends them for you.

**Where can I learn more or contribute?**

[Browse the source](https://github.com/dinkuskit/accounts) or [report an issue](https://github.com/dinkuskit/accounts/issues).
